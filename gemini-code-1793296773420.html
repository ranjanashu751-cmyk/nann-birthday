A little letter for you

Written from Jamshedpur, sent with all the warmth I can fit into words.

Dear Nann,

Happy birthday to you. 💗

I hope this new chapter of your life brings you peace in your heart, confidence in your steps, and countless little moments that remind you how beautiful life can be. I hope you keep discovering new things, chasing your dreams, and becoming the person you have always wanted to be.

I know life does not always go the way we want it to. Sometimes circumstances, especially family matters, make even the simplest things difficult. Lately, we have not been able to talk as regularly as we used to, but please never mistake the silence between our conversations for a change in how much I care about you. Not talking every day does not mean my support, affection, or good wishes for you have faded.

Even on the days when we barely get to talk, I am still wishing the best for you. I am still hoping you had a good day, that you are taking care of yourself, that your dreams are getting closer, and that life is giving you reasons to smile. Some things do not need to be said every day to remain genuine.

I cannot promise that I will always have the perfect words or that life will always be easy. But I can promise to keep trying to be someone who treats you with kindness, patience, honesty, and respect. Through the good days and the difficult ones, I hope you know that you can talk to me whenever you feel comfortable. You do not have to pretend to be okay all the time, and you never have to feel guilty for needing space when life gets overwhelming.

Distance, busy days, and circumstances may change how often we talk, but they do not have to erase the care we have built. I hope we continue to understand each other, grow as individuals, and appreciate the little moments we do get to share.

For this new year of your life, I wish you a softer heart on difficult days, courage when things feel uncertain, confidence in your own worth, and happiness that does not depend on everything going perfectly. May you find comfort in your favourite songs, get lost in beautiful books, admire little flowers, and collect memories that make you smile long after they have passed.

Please be as gentle with yourself as you are with the people you care about. Keep growing at your own pace. Keep your dreams alive. And never forget that your happiness, your peace, and the life you are building for yourself matter so much.

I do not know what every tomorrow will look like, but I genuinely hope it brings beautiful things your way. And whenever our paths allow us to share a conversation, a laugh, or a little moment together, I hope we cherish it without feeling that we have to make up for every quiet day.

This website is only a little gift, but every word here comes from a sincere place. I hope that whenever you read it, it reminds you to smile, to believe in yourself, and to remember that even when life gets complicated, there is still so much beauty waiting for you.

Happy birthday, Nann. May October be gentle with you, may this new chapter be kind to you, and may you always find reasons to believe in beautiful beginnings. 🎂🌷

Still cheering for you, with warmth, care, and all the best wishes for your journey ahead ♡
